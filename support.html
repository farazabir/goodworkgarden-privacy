Common questions

Where is my data kept?

On your device, and nowhere else. There is no account and no server. That also means deleting the app deletes your garden, permanently — so export first if you are clearing space or switching phones. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

How do I back up or move my garden?

Settings → Export as JSON writes one file containing every good work, memory and plant, with the photos embedded, and hands it to the share sheet. Save it somewhere you trust. On the new device, Settings → Import from JSON reads it back.

Import replaces everything currently on the device rather than merging, so only import into a garden you are willing to overwrite.

Does work I did away from my phone count?

Yes. On the timer screen, choose "I already did it — just log it". It plants exactly as a finished timer does. The app is meant to record good work, not to police whether a timer was running.

My reminder never arrives.

Check that notifications are allowed for Good Work Garden in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not filtering it. Reminders are scheduled on the device itself, so they also need the app to have been opened at least once since the phone last restarted.

A plant looks wrong, or the garden will not load.

Force-quit and reopen first. If it persists, export your garden and email me the file along with what you saw — that file usually tells me exactly what went wrong.

How do I get more plants?

Finishing good work unlocks milestone species. The wheel grants the rest. Every species is modelled by hand; there are thirty.

Reporting something serious

If you have found something that loses data, or anything that looks like a privacy problem, please say so in the subject line and I will prioritise it over everything else.
